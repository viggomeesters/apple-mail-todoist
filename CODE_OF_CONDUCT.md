# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent while examining evidence, accept constructive feedback, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work. Do not publish private correspondence, credentials, personal identifiers, or security details while discussing an issue.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's security-reporting channel when confidentiality is needed, or to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens a safe and productive community.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
